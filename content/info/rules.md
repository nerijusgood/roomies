First round: week of 9 Nov Agnese, 16 Nov Noeleen + Stefano, 23 Nov Nerijus, 30 Nov Noeleen + Stefano.

1. Everything is ticked off in the app by **Sunday midnight**.
2. Noeleen and Stefano share one list. A tick by either of them counts for both.
3. Can't make it? Swap with someone and tell the group chat.
4. Running low on something? Add it to the shopping list.

## If your week isn't ticked off by Sunday midnight

The money isn't a punishment. It's so whoever does extra work gets something for it. Do your week like you're meant to and it never costs you a cent.

1. **Monday from 00:01, anyone can take it.** Write "I'll take it" in the chat. The first person to write it cleans by **Monday midnight**, and you pay them **200 kr**. On the shared week, Noeleen and Stefano pay 100 kr each.
2. **Nobody takes it, or they don't finish by Monday midnight?** You clean it yourself by **Tuesday midnight**. You still pay 200 kr, but it goes into the house kitty for cleaning stuff or things the house needs.

**Miss your next turn too?** It doubles to **400 kr** (200 kr each for Noeleen and Stefano), so skipping never gets easy. It goes back to normal once you've done your week.

Example: Nerijus doesn't do his week and Agnese takes it on Monday. He pays her 200 kr.

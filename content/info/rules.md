1. Your area is done by **Sunday 21:00**.
2. Everyone rotates to a new area every week. Over 4 weeks you do each area once.
3. Can't make it? Swap with someone and tell the group chat.
4. Running low on something? Add it to the shopping list.

**Please note:** the checklist covers the main weekly clean, but with 4 people high-use areas won't last seven days.

- You may need to vacuum the floors twice. The front door area with shoes needs to be vacuumed almost 3 times a week.
- The bathroom countertop needs to be wiped down every second day.

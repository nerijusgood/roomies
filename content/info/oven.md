---
title: How to clean the oven
icon: oven
color: butter
order: 5
---
Once a month, by whoever has the kitchen.

1. Mix baking soda and a little water into a paste.
2. Spread it inside the oven (not on the heating parts). Leave for a few hours.
3. Wipe out with a damp cloth.
4. Spray a little vinegar on any white bits, then wipe again.

Yes, but only one route pays real money: organizations that run many rooms.

Charging households or users earns pocket money that covers running costs. Selling a dashboard to coliving operators, student housing companies or kollegium administrations is the only route that can reach tens of thousands of DKK a year.

Operators (B2B): the one route that pays

36,000–72,000DKK/yr per operator with 300 rooms

The operator pays 10 to 20 DKK per room per month for one dashboard of all its houses, rules and move-ins. Residents keep using it free, with no accounts.

Growth: positive, each operator brings many homesEffort: high

Needs level 4 (kitchen mode), an admin dashboard, invoices and sales. Nobody asked an operator yet.

Recommended path

Free for homes, test operators before you build

  1. Keep it free for households, foreverFree use is how it spreads from one flat to the next.
  2. Add a donation link at level 3It covers the domain and Redis. It also shows whether people care.
  3. Pitch 3 to 5 operators on one pageColiving, student housing, kollegium administrations. Before you write any dashboard code.
  4. Gate: one operator says "send us an invoice"Polite interest does not count. Also wait until one kitchen used Roomies for a semester.

Payments and termsStripe or MobilePay Subscriptions, plus terms of service.

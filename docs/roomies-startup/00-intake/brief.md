# Intake Brief

Phase: 1, Intake (Fast Track, one round)
Project: roomies-startup
Date: 2026-10-10
Confidence: Medium for the facts about the product, Low for the facts about demand

---

## The idea

Roomies is an offline-first web app (PWA) for a shared home. Each person sees their area this week, a checklist for that area, the deadline, and who in the house is done. Areas rotate every week. There are no accounts: you pick your name and type a PIN.

Details are in [`../../overview.md`](../../overview.md), [`../../analysis.md`](../../analysis.md) and [`../../roadmap.md`](../../roadmap.md).

## Problem

In a shared home, the cleaning plan lives in people's heads, on a paper plan or in the group chat. People forget whose turn it is. "Clean the kitchen" means different things to different people. Nobody can see who already did their part.

## Trigger

The founder built Roomies for their own home in Copenhagen with three roommates. The rota started in week 41 (5 Oct 2026). The app runs locally and is not deployed yet.

## Founder

- Professional web, app and AI engineer. The founder can build and run the whole product alone.
- Solo.
- Time: evenings after work and weekends. [Assumption] This is about 5 to 10 hours per week. The founder must make sure that this number is right.
- Budget: not stated. The current setup costs €0 (Vercel Hobby and Upstash free tier).

## Market and customer

- First market: people in Copenhagen who live with roommates.
- Later: the rest of Scandinavia.
- Sub-groups to choose between: private flatshares, kollegium kitchens, co-housing, coliving operators.
- Current alternatives: paper plan on the fridge, kitchen rules PDF, Messenger group, Google Sheets, Flatastic [Data, `../../competitors.md`].

## Business

- Goal of this process: compare all four paths from `analysis.md` (personal tool, open-source template, portfolio case study, multi-home product) and pick one.
- Revenue model: not chosen.
- Main worry, in the founder's words: "What is ROI vs time and build costs."

## Customer contact

- One household (the founder's own, 4 people including the founder) uses the app. It started this week.
- Conversations with people outside the household: none reported.

## Strongest argument against

The founder named ROI. The founder can spend the same evenings on other projects or on rest. Build cost is low, because the founder is the engineer, but the time cost is real.

## Kill criterion (draft)

[Assumption] The founder must agree to this criterion. Run a short test with homes outside the founder's own. If no group of homes uses Roomies every week without help from the founder, stop the product path. Roomies then stays a household tool, a template and a case study.

## Mode

Fast Track. Reason: limited time, a small and price-sensitive market, and competitor research already done.

---

## Flags

Red Flags:
- None identified

Yellow Flags:
- No customer contact outside the founder's own home. The only user group is the founder and their roommates, and they are biased.
- The app has run for less than one week. After the first month, the household can stop using it. Nobody knows yet.
- The founder's main worry is ROI, but there is no revenue model yet.

## Pivot notes (2026-10-10)

The founder changed the goal after the brainstorm. Roomies is a hobby project. The founder's time does not count as a cost, and the project does not need to earn money. The founder is a developer and designer, so build and design cost nothing. The new questions are: what can the project achieve, how to position it, which features to build, and what it costs to run. The ROI math in `brainstorm.md` stays as a record, but it no longer decides the path. See `../02-strategy/hobby-direction.md`.

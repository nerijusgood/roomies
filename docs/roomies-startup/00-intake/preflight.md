# Pre-Flight Check

Phase: 0.5, Pre-Flight Check
Project: roomies-startup
Date: 2026-10-10
Confidence: Medium

---

## Result

No finding stops the project. One finding sets the tone: the market leader is not an app.

## Dominant solution

No dominant chore app exists in Denmark [Data, `../../competitors.md`]. Flatastic is the closest competitor. It has 12 ratings in the Danish App Store [Data, AppFollow]. Sweepy and Tody are built for one home and lock sharing behind a subscription. The real competitor is the paper plan on the fridge, the kitchen rules PDF and the Messenger group [Opinion].

## Precedent failures

OurHome, a family chore app from Australia, is discontinued. The apps are gone and the website is no longer secure. No public reason was found [Data, AlternativeTo, JustUseApp]. MIT Technology Review wrote in spring 2022 that chore apps often do not reduce the real load of housework [Data, Tier 2]. Roommate startups that shut down (Roommates by Apartment List in 2014, HomeShare, MyRoomie in 2022) did roommate matching, not chores. They failed on funding and technical problems [Data, Tier 2/3]. They are not a direct precedent.

## Regulatory

No legal blocker found. GDPR applies because Roomies stores first names and tick times. The data is small and there are no emails or accounts. A short privacy notice is enough at the start [Opinion].

---

## Flags

Red Flags:
- None identified

Yellow Flags:
- Chore apps have a known pattern: people install them and stop opening them. OurHome is gone and the reason is unknown.

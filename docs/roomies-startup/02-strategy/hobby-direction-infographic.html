Positioning

Do not fight Flatastic. Replace the paper plan on the fridge.

Today: fridge plan + group chat

  • Nobody sees who is done
  • Out of date after someone moves
  • "Clean the kitchen" means different things
  • Reminders in the chat feel like nagging
→

Roomies: the fridge plan, made live

  • No accounts: a link and a 4-digit PIN
  • A checklist that says what "done" means here
  • Everyone sees who is done, live
  • Offline, free, no ads, friendly tone
Draft one-liner Your fridge cleaning plan, on everyone's phone. No sign-up. Rengøringsplanen fra køleskabet, nu på alles telefon. Uden login.

Target: shared homes of 3 to 16 equals with a weekly rotation, where people move often. Proof to get: one kollegium kitchen that uses it for a full semester.

Risks

What can stop it

People stop opening itChore apps lose users after a few weeks. Test reminders and the fridge poster.
Setup takes too longIf paper is faster, homes stay on paper.
Abuse on a public serviceKeep homes private by link and code. Rate-limit home creation.
Personal dataNames and tick times fall under GDPR. A privacy page and a "delete this home" button cover it.
Next steps

From your home to the next one

  1. Ship level 1Deploy, change the PINs, reminders, swaps in the app.
  2. Run it at home for 4 weeksWrite down who opens it without a reminder from you.
  3. Give it to 2 outside groupsOne friend's flatshare and one kollegium kitchen rep.
  4. Gate: build level 3?After 4 weeks, if at least one of them still uses it, yes.

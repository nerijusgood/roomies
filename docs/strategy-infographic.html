Unique selling point

Faster, cheaper and easier, in that order

Faster about 10 sec to join

The QR poster turns the fridge plan into the way in. Scan, pick your name, set a PIN. No app store, no email.

Cheaper €0 for flats

Flats of up to six people are free forever, with no ads. Sweepy charges about $20 a year to share. Flatastic charges about $35 a year to remove ads.

Easier da + en

Danish for the locals, English for the many international students. A gentle tone with no leaderboard, which fits Nordic ideas of fairness.

The problem it solves

Kitchens change people every semester

In a kollegium, someone moves in or out almost every month. Every app that needs accounts and invites loses people at each change. The fridge plan goes out of date. Roomies only needs a new name on the list.

This is the core reason a kitchen picks Roomies over Flatastic.

What can go wrong

Risks to plan for

iPhone push needs home-screen installShow install help in the join flow. If too many people skip it, wrap the web app as a store app later.
The champion moves outKitchens lose the person who set it up. Let any member edit people, and keep setup under two minutes.
Building sales are slowOperators decide slowly. Start the first conversation during the pilot, not after.
Students will not payThat is why flats stay free and kitchens pay from a shared fund, not from one person.

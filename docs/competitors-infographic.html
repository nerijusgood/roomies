The real rival

Habits, not apps

Fridge plan

+Free, everyone sees it

−Does not show what is done. Out of date after move-outs.

Kitchen rules PDF

+Official in every kollegium

−Says what to do, not who does it this week

Messenger group

+Everyone already has it

−The plan gets lost. Reminders feel like nagging.

Google Sheets

+Free and flexible

−Bad on a phone. One person keeps it alive.

Best fit

The kollegium kitchen

  • ↻Rotation is already the rule. The weekly kitchen clean moves between rooms, the same model as Roomies.
  • ✓Everyone must take part. Seeing who is done is the whole point.
  • ⇄People move every semester. No accounts means a new roommate joins with one link and a PIN.
  • ✎Each kitchen votes on its own rules. Markdown checklists can copy them word for word.

GJK kollegium kitchen rules: weekly clean rotates between rooms, everyone takes part, extra rules are voted at the kitchen meeting.

To win kitchens, build in this order

Four steps

  1. RemindersEvery competitor has them. Saturday morning and Sunday afternoon.
  2. Danish textThe fridge plan is in Danish. Roomies is not.
  3. People and swaps in the appNo developer needed when someone moves in or is away.
  4. Setup page for a new kitchenMore people than areas already works.ready

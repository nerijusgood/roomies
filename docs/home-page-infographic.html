The main recommendation

Make the home page a single scroll that shows the app, not a website with many pages.

Your real competitor is the paper plan on the fridge. Nobody reads a website before they use a fridge plan. So the page has one job: make a visitor try the demo or ask for Roomies for their home in under a minute. About and Contact are a section and a footer line, not pages.

Where the page lives

Three ways to place it next to the app

Today the app owns /. The PWA starts at start_url: '/', and the service worker sends every path to the app. So the home page needs its own place.

Recommended

A. Home at the root, app under a path

roomies.dk/roomies.dk/app
  • One domain, one deploy, one Vite build with two entry pages
  • Fits the multi-home plan later (/app remembers your home)
  • Cheap now, because no phone has installed the app yet
  • Move start_url and the service worker scope to /app

B. Two subdomains

roomies.dkapp.roomies.dk
  • Clean split, the home page can be any static site
  • The service worker never touches the home page
  • Two projects and two deploys to keep in sync
  • More setup than a hobby needs at level 2

C. No home page, a welcome state

roomies.dk/(same app)
  • Least work: the login screen gets a "What is this?" card
  • Enough for level 1 and early level 2
  • The whole app bundle loads for a curious visitor
  • Hard to grow into a Danish page or a kitchen page
Decide this before the first deploy. If you change start_url after your roomies add the app to their home screens, their icons can keep opening the old address. Today nobody has installed it, so a move is free.
One question before the design

What is the home page's first job?

The answer decides the main button, which blocks come first, and when to build it.
